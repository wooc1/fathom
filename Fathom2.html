How scoring works

You get seven prompts and 30 seconds each. Type one word or short phrase. Every answer scores from 0 to 100 on how rarely people write it, and every point is worth 10 metres of depth.

Every answer is checked against a dictionary of what actually belongs in the category. Made-up words, typos and things that don’t fit are rejected, and you can try again while the clock runs.

Points come from how rarely the word shows up in English text (Wikipedia, subtitles, news, books, the web). “Apple” is everywhere and scores close to nothing. A fruit most people have never written down scores close to 100.

The daily dive is the same seven prompts for everyone on a given date. Scores measure how unusual the word is in print, not what other players typed.
